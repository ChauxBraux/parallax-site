Fatal Minneapolis Shooting by Federal Agents Sparks Calls for DHS Secretary's Resignation

A fatal shooting by federal agents in Minneapolis on Saturday has ignited a political firestorm, with Alex Pretti, a 37-year-old ICU nurse, killed during an immigration enforcement operation. An initial Department of Homeland Security report to Congress revealed that two officers—one Border Patrol agent and one CBP officer—fired shots at Pretti, though the preliminary review made no mention of him attacking officers or threatening them with a weapon, contradicting initial administration claims. The incident follows another recent federal shooting death in Minnesota and has prompted House Democrats to demand President Trump fire Homeland Security Secretary Kristi Noem or face impeachment proceedings, with more than three-quarters of the House Democratic caucus now co-sponsoring articles of impeachment. Trump deployed Border Czar Tom Homan to Minnesota for a "productive" meeting with Governor Tim Walz and Minneapolis Mayor Jacob Frey to de-escalate tensions. Notably, some Republican senators, including Thom Tillis of North Carolina who voted to confirm Noem, have broken ranks and called for her resignation, marking the first significant GOP fracture over immigration policy in Trump's second term.
