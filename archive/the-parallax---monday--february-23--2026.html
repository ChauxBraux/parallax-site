Supreme Court Strikes Down Trump's Emergency Tariffs, Administration Pivots to 15% Global Levy

The ruling does not resolve the question of refunds for businesses that already paid the disputed tariffs, leaving that issue to lower courts. In response, Trump's administration announced a new 15% universal tariff on all imports and pledged to maintain existing bilateral trade deals with China, the European Union, Japan, South Korea, and other partners. The decision has created uncertainty in financial markets and left businesses facing potential litigation to recover tariff payments.
